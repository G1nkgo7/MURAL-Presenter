# Evidence Policy

## Source Priority
1. Primary sources (original papers, official institutions, artist statements)
2. Secondary sources (academic reviews, reputable journalism)
3. Tertiary sources (encyclopedias, aggregated databases)

## Evidence Quality
- Prefer sources with clear authorship and date
- Cross-check facts across at least 2 independent sources when possible
- Flag single-source claims as `single_source`

## Conflict Handling
- When sources conflict, record both with confidence levels
- Do not silently resolve conflicts; flag for Orchestrator decision

## Knowledge Gate
- `sufficient=true` requires no critical gaps in core facts
- Non-critical gaps may be marked `unresolved` and accepted
