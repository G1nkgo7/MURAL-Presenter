# Creative Direction for Strong Authoring Models

## 1. Start with one visual thesis

Ask what world the audience should enter and which physical material, real interface, place, era, object, data behavior, or emotional temperature best represents the topic. Turn the answer into one executable `visual_thesis`. “Modern, clean, professional, premium” is not a thesis.

The thesis should determine color, type, image behavior, line language, rhythm, and special pages. Derive a `palette_anchor` from actual content rather than default ivory/navy or neon technology. Define one signature motif that carries real meaning rather than fake metadata.

## 2. Stable language, changing pages

Keep type roles, color semantics, base grid, image treatment, line/radius behavior, source format, and motif logic stable. Vary first focus, primary/secondary ratio, reading direction, medium, density, whitespace position, and chapter state. Adjacent pages should change at least one of these rather than swapping only copy and images.

Use two to four page families: editorial evidence, image narrative, mechanism, and data focus are common examples. Cover, divider, and closing are three actions from one design DNA, not normal pages with different backgrounds.

## 3. Typography before containers

Give titles a voice through scale, weight, width, line breaks, and contrast with body. Give body a clear reading axis and numbers an independent role. Build hierarchy with type before adding panels. Cards are for true parallel structure; otherwise prefer open grids, rules, fields, or whitespace. Do not give Chinese labels fake technical authority through Latin all-caps or monospace styling.

## 4. Color comes from content

Map the palette anchor into field/surface/ink/muted/accent roles. Accent marks the current conclusion, risk, choice, variable, or chapter identity. Professional does not mean ivory and navy; technology does not mean blue-purple glow; academic does not mean a white document with small headings. Preserve source color when it carries identity or data meaning.

## 5. The primary visual carries information

At least one bitmap, chart, SVG, Canvas, or typographic event must dominate the reading. Tiny thumbnails, decorative icons, empty panels, and background circles do not count.

Preserve identifiable faces, hands, product silhouettes, logos, artwork subjects, axes, and legends. Crop paper Figures to the visual subject while retaining required panels and labels and excluding body text. Draw relationships and direction before nodes and labels in mechanisms. Let images carry object/evidence/atmosphere and HTML carry accurate language.

## 6. Live rhythm

Build tension and release: context → tension → evidence → mechanism → result → choice/action → closure. Visual peaks should correspond to narrative peaks. A breathing page may be sparse but still needs a dominant title, image, number, or motif. Empty space without direction is unfinished, not minimal.

## 7. Contact-sheet rejection criteria

Reject repeated split layouts or equal-card grids; a cover that reads like a normal title page; a closing that becomes three takeaway cards; images or charts reduced to corner decoration; content trapped in a narrow band with ownerless space; academic pages that imitate paper screenshots; repeated generic glows/fake IDs; and a deck that is error-free but has no describable visual thesis.

Review the shared causes with the greatest effect on the whole deck: title voice, primary-visual weight, repeated silhouettes, canvas drift, and missing evidence. Do not damage an effective composition to clear a diagnostic with no visible consequence.
